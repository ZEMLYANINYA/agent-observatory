# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the experimental methodology used by Agent Observatory.

The goal is to make observations reproducible and to clearly distinguish measured behavior from assumptions or interpretation.

## Experimental Model

The project follows a simple research loop:

```text
Question
   ↓
Hypothesis
   ↓
Controlled experiment
   ↓
Telemetry collection
   ↓
Correlation
   ↓
Observation
   ↓
Finding
   ↓
Limitation
   ↓
Next question
